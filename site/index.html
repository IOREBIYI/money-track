MoneyTrack

Track spending, set budgets. Your data stays on your phone.

Download APK View source

Features

  • Income and expenses with category, date and note
  • 39 currencies (display only)
  • Monthly budgets with progress bars
  • Reports: spending by category, 6-month income vs. expenses
  • CSV export and restore
  • Email sign-in with in-app account deletion
  • No ads, no analytics. Transactions never leave your device

Requires Android 8.0+. Built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Room and Firebase Authentication.
