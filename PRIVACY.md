# MoneyTrack Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

**In short:** your transactions and budgets stay on your phone. To sign in, MoneyTrack uses an account made of your email address and a password, handled by Google Firebase Authentication. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## What we collect
- **Email address and password.** Used to create your account, sign you in, and send password-reset emails. Your password is stored by Firebase as a salted hash; we can never see it.
- **Account ID and sign-in tokens.** A random Firebase user ID and tokens that keep you signed in.
- **Technical sign-in data.** When you sign in, Firebase processes technical information such as your IP address, device and app details, and timestamps, for security and abuse prevention. We don't use it for anything else.

## What stays on your device
Your transactions, budgets, categories and currency choice are stored **only on your device**. They are never sent to us or to any server. The internet is used only to sign you in, create an account, send password-reset emails, and delete your account.

## Who handles your data
Google LLC provides Firebase Authentication and processes the account data above on our behalf. See [Firebase's privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy). We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for marketing.

## How long we keep it
Your account data is kept until you delete your account.

## Deleting your account and data
- **In the app:** open **Settings → Delete my account** and confirm with your password. This permanently deletes your email address and account ID.
- **Without the app:** email orebiyimayowa3@gmail.com from your registered address and ask us to delete your account. See [ACCOUNT_DELETION.md](ACCOUNT_DELETION.md).
- Transactions and budgets live only on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes them.

## Export
If you export a CSV file, it is saved where you choose. You control that file.

## Security
Account data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

## Children
MoneyTrack is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we don't knowingly create accounts for them.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the date above will change and the new version will be published here.
