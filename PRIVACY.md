# MoneyTrack Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

MoneyTrack does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

- **Data storage:** All transactions and budgets are stored locally on your device only.
- **No network access:** The app does not request internet permission and cannot send data anywhere.
- **No accounts, ads or analytics:** None are used.
- **Export:** If you export a CSV file, it is saved where you choose. You control that file.
- **Deletion:** Uninstalling the app removes all its data from your device.
