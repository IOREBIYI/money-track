# Delete your MoneyTrack account

You can delete your MoneyTrack account and the data tied to it at any time.

## In the app (fastest)
1. Open MoneyTrack and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings → Delete my account**.
3. Enter your password and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

## If you can't use the app
Email **orebiyimayowa3@gmail.com** from the email address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my MoneyTrack account". We will delete the account and confirm by email, normally within 30 days.

## What gets deleted
- Your email address
- Your account ID and sign-in details

## What is not affected
Your transactions and budgets are stored only on your phone and are not part of your account. They stay on the device until you uninstall MoneyTrack or clear its storage in Android settings (**Settings → Apps → MoneyTrack → Storage → Clear storage**). Export a CSV backup first if you want to keep them.

See also the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).
